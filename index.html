Bed placement — south head
Compliant
SW (Nairutya) zone is governed by the earth element — ideal for the head of household's bedroom. Sleeping with head pointing south aligns with Earth's magnetic field and promotes restful sleep.
Manasara Ch. 42
✓
Kitchen in SE zone
Compliant
SE is ruled by Agni (fire) — the ideal placement for all cooking spaces. SE-facing kitchens in India receive morning light, maximising natural ventilation and reducing humidity.
Manasara Ch. 11
!
Mirror placement advisory
Review
The north wall is ideal for mirrors — associated with Kubera, the deity of wealth. We avoid suggesting mirrors on south or west bedroom walls in all our designs.
Mayamata Ch. 12

When our AI detects a structural beam above the sleeping position from your room photo, we immediately suggest a canopy bed or fabric drape to neutralise this without structural changes.

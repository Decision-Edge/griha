Our approach

Why you can trust
Griha's recommendations

We don't claim to replace centuries of tradition. We make it accessible, accurate, and actionable for modern Indian home buyers.

🧭
Backed by certified Vastu consultants
Every Vastu rule in Griha's engine is reviewed and approved by consultants affiliated with the Indian Association of Vastu Architects (IAVA). Our "Expert in Residence" signs off on our analysis logic before any recommendation reaches you.
📜
Sourced from classical Sanskrit texts
Griha's Vastu intelligence is rooted in the Manasara (c. 5th century CE), the Mayamata (c. 8th century CE), and the Samarangana Sutradhara (11th century CE). We cite chapter and verse — not internet summaries.
🔬
Bridged with modern science
Where Vastu principles align with environmental psychology, circadian rhythm research, or BIS National Building Code guidelines, we surface that connection — so sceptical buyers and traditional families alike find value in every recommendation.
Our honest positioning: Griha surfaces Vastu intelligence — we are a platform, not a personal consulting practice. For complex cases or large homes, we recommend working with a certified Vastu consultant alongside our tool. We can connect you with one.
Classical sources we cite
c. 5th CE
Manasara
The most comprehensive classical Vastu text. Covers building orientation, room placement, and proportions. Primary source for our structural Vastu rules. English translation by P.K. Acharya.
c. 8th CE
Mayamata
Tamil classical text on architecture and Vastu. Particularly relevant for South Indian buyers. Translation by Bruno Dagen (French Institute of Pondicherry).
11th CE
Samarangana Sutradhara
Written by King Bhoja of Dhar. Covers directional energies, compass orientation, and interior arrangements. Source for our Vastu zone mapping logic.
Modern
IIT Research + BIS NBC 2016
IIT Kharagpur and IIT Roorkee research on the climatic and scientific basis of Vastu principles, cross-referenced with the National Building Code of India.
